正式练习前展示的完整说明

接下来，你会看到一行数字，最后一个位置是问号。请看看相邻数字怎样变化，再从四个选项中选出最符合规律的下一个数字。有的每次加同样多，有的每次乘同样的数，有的增加量会一步步变大。上面的三个例子分别演示这三种关系。正式题会混合出现，不会告诉你属于哪类。整行数字会一直留着，不用抢答；请先想清楚。点选即提交，不能回改；也可以选“这题先跳过”。想停下时可以结束，不需要猜完。每组 6 题后可以休息。请自己作答，不使用计算器，也不请别人代答；不会或不舒服时可以退出。先做三题较简单的练习，帮助熟悉规则和操作，练习不算入正式结果。

Full English instructions

You will see a row of numbers ending in a question mark. Look at how the numbers change, then choose the next number from four options. Some rows add the same amount each time, some multiply by the same number, and some have gaps that grow step by step. The three examples above show these relationships. Formal questions mix the three types without naming the rule. The whole row stays visible. Take your time and think before choosing. Your first choice submits the answer and cannot be changed. You may choose “Skip this question” or end the activity. You may rest after each group of six questions. Work on your own, without a calculator or someone answering for you. If this feels unfamiliar or uncomfortable, you may stop. First, complete three easier practice questions to learn the rules and controls. Practice does not count toward your formal result.

Examples: 3, 7, 11, 15, ? adds 4 each time, so choose 19. 2, 6, 18, 54, ? multiplies by 3 each time, so choose 162. In 2, 5, 9, 14, ?, the gaps are 3, 4 and 5. The next gap is 6, so choose 20. These are teaching examples, not formal items.

练习共 3 题，三类各 1；较小数字，不限时、逐题解释，3/3 才继续。失败、跳过或未完成后，先完整重呈说明和全部三例，再练，最多 3 轮，可退出。重练每轮另估 2–3 分钟，不包含在首次预算中。
