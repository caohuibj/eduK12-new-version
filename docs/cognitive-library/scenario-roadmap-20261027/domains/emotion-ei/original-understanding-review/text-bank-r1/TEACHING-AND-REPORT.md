# 教学、3次练习及结果文字全稿

与SCENARIOS.md中的36题同属文字审阅r1。本文件没有执行流程、自动计分或保存实现；练习的“规则答案”仅来自明示规则／事实，不是情绪专家键。

## 正式前说明：中文全文

你将阅读几段简短的人物情境。请从主人公的角度，结合这段文字说明的目标和已知信息，选择此刻更相符的感受。这里不是问你自己的心情，也不是问主人公应该怎么做。

每题有五个选择：快乐、释然、希望、其他感受更符合、信息不足，无法判断。这些感受可能重叠。请选你认为最相符的一项；不要求你认同人物的想法。其他感受更贴切时，选“其他感受更符合”；文字不足以判断时，选“信息不足，无法判断”。两者都是有效记录，不会判错。

不想判断某题，可以单独选择“跳过本题”；也可以结束本次。跳过不等于“其他感受”或“信息不足”。我们不会要求你补充自己或真实他人的私人经历。

先选一项，再按“确认这一项”。确认前可改，确认后不再回改。没有单题倒计时，不需要抢快；正式题不显示对错或推荐答案。

本研究尚未建立经过验证的专家答案键，因此不计算正确率、能力分、情商分或排名。你的选择是对有限文字的解读，不能确定真实人物只有这一种感受。

先看下面的例子，再完成3次简短练习。练习只帮助确认作答方式和事实理解，不评价你的情绪能力。3项规则都核对完成后才进入正式情境；如果有错误或未完成，会先重新显示全部说明、例子和选项解释，最多进行2组。第二组仍未完成时，本次结束，不产生正式结果；愿意的话可以以后再来。

研究版36题，含说明与练习约12–18分钟，每12题可休息；体验版12题，约6–9分钟，不额外插入固定休息。这是估时，不是截止时间；阅读较慢或重新练习可能用更久。你可以按自己的节奏完成。

## Instructions: complete English draft

You will read a series of short situations involving fictional people. Take the main character's perspective and use the goal and information stated in the text to choose the feeling that fits best at that moment. We are not asking about your own feelings or what the character should do.

Each item has five choices: Joy, Relief, Hope, Another feeling fits better, and Not enough information. Feelings can overlap. Choose the one that you think fits best; you do not have to agree with the character's goals. If a different feeling fits better, choose “Another feeling fits better.” If the text does not provide enough information, choose “Not enough information.” Both are valid responses and are not marked wrong.

If you do not want to judge an item, you may choose “Skip this item.” You may also end the session. Skipping is different from choosing another feeling or saying that information is missing. You will not be asked to describe private experiences involving yourself or real people.

Select an option, then press “Confirm this choice.” You may change it before confirming, but not afterwards. There is no item countdown and no need to hurry. Formal items do not show right or wrong feedback or a recommended answer.

A validated expert answer key has not been established for this study. We therefore do not calculate accuracy, ability, emotional-intelligence scores or rankings. Your choice is an interpretation of limited text; it cannot establish that a real person has only one feeling.

Read the example below, then complete three short practice checks. Practice helps you check the response instructions and factual information, not your emotional ability. All three rule checks must be completed before the formal situations begin. If a check is incorrect or unfinished, all instructions, the example and the option explanations are shown again before another group of three checks. There are at most two practice groups. If the second group remains unfinished, this session ends without a formal result. You may return another time if you wish.

The research version has 36 items and is estimated to take 12–18 minutes including instructions and practice, with an optional break after every 12 items. The experience version has 12 items and is estimated to take 6–9 minutes, with no additional scheduled break. These are estimates, not deadlines. Slower reading or repeated practice may take longer. Work at your own pace.

英文阅读耗时尚未测量，不能声称一定落在中文估时区间内。以上均为原创平行语言稿，待文字review；不是经验证译本。

## 选项解释／Option explanations

| 选项 | 易懂说明（中文） | English explanation |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 快乐／Joy | 你认为开心或愉快的感受最贴切。 | You think a feeling of happiness or pleasure fits best. |
| 释然／Relief | 你认为原先挂心的事减轻了、松了一口气的感受最贴切。 | You think a feeling of easing concern or being able to relax fits best. |
| 希望／Hope | 你认为对可能的好结果抱有期待的感受最贴切。 | You think looking towards a possible favourable outcome fits best. |
| 其他感受更符合／Another feeling fits better | 你认为可以作判断，但前三个词不贴切；不用另写情绪或私人经历。 | You think a judgment is possible, but the first three labels do not fit. No extra feeling or private experience needs to be written. |
| 信息不足，无法判断／Not enough information | 你认为文字没有给出足够线索。 | You think the text does not provide enough evidence. |
| 跳过本题／Skip this item | 你不提交这一题的感受判断，原因不用说明。 | You do not submit a feeling judgment for this item. No reason is required. |

解释是为减少操作和词义误解，不给每一条件指定正确标签；几个词仍可能重叠，文化与语言差异尚未研究。

## 教学例子（不进入36题）

**中文**：江禾和同伴准备周末的阅读活动。她不想借来的投影仪晚到而影响活动。刚才同伴已经收到投影仪，并确认设备能正常工作。江禾知道这件原先可能影响活动的事已经解决了。

**English**: Jiang and a friend are preparing a weekend reading session. She does not want a borrowed projector to arrive late and disrupt the activity. Her friend has now received it and checked that it works. Jiang knows that this potential problem has been resolved.

**题问**：从这些线索看，江禾此刻哪种感受更相符？／Based on this information, which feeling best fits Jiang at this moment?

**可选**：快乐Joy；释然Relief；希望Hope；其他感受更符合Another feeling fits better；信息不足Not enough information。另有跳过Skip this item。

**讲解**：人物是江禾，不是答题者；设备已经收到且能用，是已确认信息。你可以认为释然更贴切，也可以认为其他解释更合理。本例不给情绪标准答案，也不把某个选项称为更好。图稿选中“释然”只演示选择后确认，不作为正确反馈。

**Explanation**: Consider Jiang, not yourself. The equipment has arrived and works; those facts are confirmed. You may think relief fits best, or you may find another interpretation more reasonable. This example gives no standard emotion answer and does not call one choice better. “Relief” is selected in the design image only to illustrate the response controls.

## 练习1：人物视角／Whose perspective?

**中文**：小竹正在等一个活动的报名结果。她想知道自己是否能参加。这里要判断谁的感受？

**English**: Zhu is waiting for the result of an activity registration. She wants to know whether she can attend. Whose feeling are you asked to consider?

**选项**：小竹／Zhu；你自己／Yourself。

**规则答案**：小竹。依据是说明明确要求人物视角，不是专家情绪判断。

**完成反馈**：“这一项核对完成。请从文字中的人物视角判断，不是报告你自己的心情。”／“This check is complete. Consider the character in the text, rather than reporting your own feelings.”

**未对上时**：“这里问的是小竹。我们先再看一遍说明和例子，再核对作答方式。”／“This question is about Zhu. Let us revisit the instructions and example before checking the response rules again.”

## 练习2：确定的信息／Confirmed information

**中文**：一条通知写着“可能周五送到，目前尚未确认”。送达日期已经确定了吗？

**English**: A message says, “It may arrive on Friday; this has not been confirmed.” Is the arrival date confirmed?

**选项**：已确定／Confirmed；尚未确定／Not confirmed。

**事实答案**：尚未确定。只检查“可能”和“确认”的文字事实，不判任何情绪。

**完成反馈**：“这一项核对完成。‘可能’表示仍会变化，和已经确认不同。”／“This check is complete. ‘May’ leaves the outcome open; it is different from confirmation.”

**未对上时**：“通知还没有确认日期。我们先再看一遍说明和例子，再核对已知信息。”／“The message has not confirmed the date. Let us revisit the instructions and example before checking what is known.”

## 练习3：记录方式／Recording your judgment

**中文**：如果你认为人物的感受可以判断，但快乐、释然、希望都不贴切，应选哪一项？

**English**: If you think you can judge the character's feeling, but joy, relief and hope do not fit, which option should you use?

**选项**：其他感受更符合／Another feeling fits better；信息不足／Not enough information；跳过本题／Skip this item。

**规则答案**：其他感受更符合。题面明确说可以判断但前三词不贴切；不是要求人物一定应有某种情绪。

**完成反馈**：“这一项核对完成。‘其他’表示前三词不贴切；‘信息不足’表示线索不够；‘跳过’表示不提交判断。”／“This check is complete. ‘Another feeling’ means the first three labels do not fit; ‘Not enough information’ means evidence is missing; ‘Skip’ means no judgment is submitted.”

**未对上时**：“这种情况用‘其他感受更符合’记录。我们先再看一遍选项解释和例子，不会把它判成情绪能力差。”／“Use ‘Another feeling fits better’ in this case. Let us revisit the option explanations and example. This is not a judgment of emotional ability.”

## 教学结束、重呈与结束用语

- **全部核对完成**：“3项规则已核对完成。正式情境没有情绪标准答案反馈，你可以按自己的节奏开始。”／“All three rule checks are complete. Formal situations do not provide standard emotion-answer feedback. Begin when you are ready.”
- **首组有错误或未完成**：“作答方式还没有全部核对完成。请再读完整说明、例子和选项解释，随后重新进行3次短练习。”／“The response rules have not all been checked yet. Read the full instructions, example and option explanations again, then repeat the three short practice checks.” 重呈不能只放“再试一次”按钮或跳过例子。
- **第二组仍未完成**：“本次练习先到这里，没有进入正式情境，也不会生成正式结果。你可以稍后再读说明后重新开始。”／“Practice ends here for this session. No formal situations were started and no formal result will be produced. You may read the instructions and start again another time.”
- **主动结束**：“是否结束本次？已确认、跳过和未完成的情境会分别记录，不会把未完成部分判为错误。”／“End this session? Confirmed, skipped and unfinished situations will remain separate; unfinished items will not be treated as wrong.” 这是拟文本，不是已实现的弹窗或保存保证。

练习逐项给规则解释，全部3项完成且3/3规则／事实核对正确才继续。最多2组；练习不进入正式分母，不换算为能力分。意外离开导致练习未完成，返回后先完整重呈，不静默进入正式题。

## 缺失、中断与首次确认：文字层面的约定

“其他”和“信息不足”是已确认回应；跳过是明确不提交判断；页面中断前只有高亮但没有确认，不算已回答。未出现的后续题保持“未呈现”。三者不互换，也不补成0分。

首个有效确认锁定唯一回应，重复确认不增加人数、题数或条件分母。无单题超时，停留较久不自动记遗漏。主动结束或宿主中断后不自动补答、重开或伪造保存成功；当前文字稿没有任何实际数据采集或保存行为。

每条件的已确认五类次数之和等于该条件有效确认数；该分母为0时比例为null。有有效确认但某选项从未被选，计数0才是真零。条件差如未来研究计算，需明确前项减后项，保留正负号；有一侧无分母即null。此处未实现这些运算，也没有权威能力计分公式可用。

## 报告全文示意：三态都不输出能力分

下面所有数字均为虚构排版例。实际报告必须依据真实、可核对且版本冻结的记录；不得把此文字当成已经有参与者数据或报告落库。

### 记录完整：interpretable

**中文**：谢谢你完成12个情境。你在这些文字中选择了：快乐4次、释然3次、希望3次、其他感受1次、信息不足1次。这些次数只描述本次判断。我们尚未建立经过验证的专家答案键，因此不计算正确率、能力分或情商分。日常交流中，可以先确认对方的目标和已知信息，再询问对方的感受。短文字只提供有限线索，多种理解都可能合理；这次结果不能代表你在其他情境中的表现。

**English**: Thank you for completing 12 situations. For these texts, you chose Joy 4 times, Relief 3 times, Hope 3 times, Another feeling once, and Not enough information once. These counts describe your judgments in this session only. A validated expert answer key has not been established, so we do not calculate accuracy, ability or emotional-intelligence scores. In everyday conversations, clarify the person's goal and what is known, then ask how they feel. Short texts provide limited evidence, and more than one interpretation may be reasonable. This result does not establish how you would respond in other situations.

完整仅指12个计划项都得到可核对的确认选择，其他／信息不足次数高也不判无效。此状态不代表测验有效度已验证。

### 记录不完整：limited

**中文**：本次在中途结束。原计划12个情境，其中7项已确认、1项跳过、1项中断，另有3项未呈现。记录不完整，因此不生成整次选择分布。已确认的内容与跳过、未完成部分分开保留，不把未完成当作答错。愿意的话，你可以另开新的一次。

**English**: The session ended early. Of 12 planned situations, 7 were confirmed, 1 was skipped, 1 was interrupted, and 3 were not presented. The record is incomplete, so no overall choice distribution is shown. Confirmed responses remain separate from skipped and unfinished items; unfinished items are not treated as wrong. You may start a new session if you wish.

不显示全局五类分布、条件高低或整体均值；图片和下载执行相同限制，不从原始记录另算一个整体结果。

### 无可核正式记录：invalid

**中文**：未获得可核对的正式记录，暂不能生成本次选择总结。你可以重新阅读说明后开始新的一次。

**English**: No verifiable formal record is available, so a choice summary cannot be produced. You can read the instructions and start a new session.

不补0次表现，不生成表现图或表现下载。练习未完成另用练习结束说明，不把练习状态冒充正式能力结果。报告保存与完整原件归档不是同一个成功状态；此稿没有实现其中任何一项。
