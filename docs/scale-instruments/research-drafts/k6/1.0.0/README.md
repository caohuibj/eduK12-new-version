# K6 official Mandarin research materials, version intent 1.0.0

Unregistered research materials, not executable/import content. No instrument.ts, runtime registration, CI, commit, push or participant data.

Selected form: exact six Q1a–f symptoms from author-hosted K6+ forms, past 30 days. This is NOT the complete K6+ questionnaire. Supplementary Q2–Q6 and demographic fields are excluded. No attention or safety question is inserted.

## Files
- content-draft.json: complete selected six English and Mandarin items, prompts, instructions, choices and source identity.
- source-and-rights.json: frozen source SHA, copyright, grant, literature and non-executable threshold metadata. Hong Kong AUC numeric claim omitted pending primary verification after reviewer HTTP403.
- scoring-contract.json: explicit printed 1–5 to 4–0 conversion versus already-semantic 0–4 identity path. Never double reverse. All items distress-keyed. Total 0–24.
- golden-fixtures.json: synthetic arithmetic, each missing item and invalid/duplicate input cases. No clinical classifications.
- report-design.json: descriptive total and limitations; unconditional support text outside original items.
- validation.json: exactly one pure JSON/provenance/math check, not runtime verification.

Missing -> no total is a proposed platform policy, not an author-prescribed universal missing rule. Skip/exit remains available. No imputation or forced disclosure.

Thresholds remain original English study metadata only. The 2003 calibration used emotionally worst month in the previous 12 months, not this 30-day Mandarin form. No automatic bands, screen-positive labels, norms or diagnostic claims. Chinese study matching remains open. Journal articles and official source PDFs are links only; no files hosted in this package.

Adult-facing audience, administration instruction display, honest QC and separate scientific maturity, consent/privacy/retention, and local validity remain deployment gates. No human approval is invented. Preview raster asset remains pending.

## Frozen verification scope
K6 docs only → parse JSON, bind full source text/copyright/SHA and check two scoring modes plus invalid/missing synthetic cases → one local pure-data execution → preserve evidence, no app suite or CI → about one minute. This cannot establish rendering, runner behavior or psychometric validity.
