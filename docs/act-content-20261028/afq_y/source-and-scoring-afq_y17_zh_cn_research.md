# AFQ-Y 原文和计分核对

Greco, L. A., Lambert, W., & Baer, R. A. (2008). Psychological inflexibility in childhood and adolescence: Development and evaluation of the Avoidance and Fusion Questionnaire for Youth. Psychological Assessment, 20, 93-102.

来源：https://www.ruthbaer.com/academics/AFQ-Y.pdf

## 题文

|---|---|---|
| AFQY_01 | My life won't be good until I feel happy. | 除非我感到开心，否则我的生活不会好。 |
| AFQY_02 | My thoughts and feelings mess up my life. | 我的想法和感受把我的生活搞得一团糟。 |
| AFQY_03 | If I feel sad or afraid, then something must be wrong with me. | 如果我感到难过或害怕，那一定是我出了问题。 |
| AFQY_04 | The bad things I think about myself must be true. | 我对自己想的那些坏话一定是真的。 |
| AFQY_05 | I don't try out new things if I'm afraid of messing up. | 如果害怕搞砸，我就不去尝试新事物。 |
| AFQY_06 | I must get rid of my worries and fears so I can have a good life. | 我必须摆脱担忧和恐惧，才能过上好日子。 |
| AFQY_07 | I do all I can to make sure I don't look dumb in front of other people. | 我会竭尽所能，确保自己不在别人面前出丑。 |
| AFQY_08 | I try hard to erase hurtful memories from my mind. | 我努力把伤人的记忆从脑子里抹掉。 |
| AFQY_09 | I can't stand to feel pain or hurt in my body. | 我受不了身体上的疼痛或难受。 |

| AFQY_11 | I push away thoughts and feelings that I don't like. | 我把不喜欢的想法和感受推开。 |
| AFQY_12 | I stop doing things that are important to me whenever I feel bad. | 一感觉不好，我就停下对我重要的事。 |
| AFQY_13 | I do worse in school when I have thoughts that make me feel sad. | 当我有让我难过的想法时，我在学校表现得更差。 |
| AFQY_14 | I say things to make me sound cool. | 我说一些让自己显得很酷的话。 |
| AFQY_15 | I wish I could wave a magic wand to make all my sadness go away. | 我希望自己能挥一挥魔法棒，让所有悲伤都消失。 |
| AFQY_16 | I am afraid of my feelings. | 我害怕自己的感受。 |
| AFQY_17 | I can't be a good friend when I feel upset. | 当我心烦意乱时，我没办法做好朋友。 |

## 量尺

英文锚点为 Not at all True / A little True / Pretty True / True / Very True。

## 计分

- 十七题求和，范围 0–68。分数越高表示越多报告被想法与感受缠住、回避内心体验并因此影响重要活动。不是诊断，也不给学生贴标签。
