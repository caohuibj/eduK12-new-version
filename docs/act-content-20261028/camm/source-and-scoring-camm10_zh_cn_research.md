# CAMM 原文和计分核对

Greco, L., Baer, R. A., & Smith, G. T. (2011). Assessing mindfulness in children and adolescents: Development and validation of the child and adolescent mindfulness measure (CAMM). Psychological Assessment, 23, 606-614.

来源：https://ruthbaer.com/academics/CAMM.pdf

## 题文

| 题码 | 原文 | 中文工作译稿 |
|---|---|---|
| CAMM_01 | I get upset with myself for having feelings that don't make sense. | 我会为自己有一些说不通的情绪而生自己的气。 |
| CAMM_02 | At school, I walk from class to class without noticing what I'm doing. | 在学校里，我从一个教室走到另一个教室，却没留意自己在做什么。 |
| CAMM_03 | I keep myself busy so I don't notice my thoughts or feelings. | 我让自己忙个不停，好不去注意自己的想法或感受。 |
| CAMM_04 | I tell myself that I shouldn't feel the way I'm feeling. | 我告诉自己，我不应该有现在这种感受。 |
| CAMM_05 | I push away thoughts that I don't like. | 我把不喜欢的想法推开。 |
| CAMM_06 | It's hard for me to pay attention to only one thing at a time. | 我很难一次只专注于一件事。 |
| CAMM_07 | I get upset with myself for having certain thoughts. | 我会为自己有某些念头而生自己的气。 |
| CAMM_08 | I think about things that have happened in the past instead of thinking about things that are happening right now. | 我总想着过去发生的事，而不是留意当下正在发生的事。 |
| CAMM_09 | I think that some of my feelings are bad and that I shouldn't have them. | 我觉得有些情绪是不好的，我不应该有它们。 |
| CAMM_10 | I stop myself from having feelings that I don't like. | 我阻止自己产生不喜欢的情绪。 |
