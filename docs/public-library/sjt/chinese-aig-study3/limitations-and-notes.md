# Limitations and source fidelity

- Only Study3: 40 items, 160 options, five facets. No Study1 comparator or Study2 bank material included.
- Study3 research: 443 valid participants, ages18–58; criterion subsample130, two-week retest80. Predominantly highly educated sample, not population norms. Article §6, source link https://arxiv.org/abs/2412.12144v5 .
- Facet mapping source: supplement §1.4/Table S1 pp10–11 and article §6.2.1. Domain labels retained only as original metadata, not claims to full domain coverage.
- Standalone author introduction and missing-data rule not located. New platform introduction/report/complete-facet guardrail are explicitly marked as platform additions.
- No clinical, educational-selection or employment-selection use; no cutoffs, total, percentiles or source-unjustified recommendations.
- The original Chinese scenario and response_options_and_scoring_verbatim strings are immutable in this draft. Structured option text preserves whitespace after the original A./B./C./D. labels. No typography, grammar or perspective changes silently applied.
- Known visible wording retained, not corrected: O_07 uses “他们” for ideas; C_08 scenario says “伙伴” but option A says “伴侣”, and options shift to second person. These are source text, not transcription fixes.
- A/B=1 is trait-keyed, not a correct-answer or ethical endorsement. This is especially important for privacy, overtime, disagreement and interpersonal-boundary scenarios.
- Independent reviewer additionally flagged E_03 “你/您” mixed address; O_03 “做为” and option D “重新来过一个…方案”; A_04 “影响了你的集中”. These remain verbatim; this note is not an author erratum.
