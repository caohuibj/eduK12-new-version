## Instructions / 作答说明

**New editorial wording, not source verbatim. 新编说明，并非原文逐字引述。**

Read each of the 19 situations in the context of undergraduate engineering study in the United States. For each situation, select the response you would be MOST likely to take and a DIFFERENT response you would be LEAST likely to take. Answer according to what you would actually be likely to do, rather than what seems ideal. There are no objectively correct or incorrect responses. Complete both selections for every situation. This draft summarizes response preferences; it is not an ability test or a clinical assessment. The Chinese translation has not been validated.

请结合美国本科工程教育的情境，阅读以下 19 个情境。每个情境请选择一项你最可能采取的做法，以及另一项你最不可能采取的做法；两项选择不能相同。请根据自己实际可能采取的做法回答，而不是选择看起来最理想的做法。这里没有客观的正确或错误答案。每个情境的两项选择都需要完成。本草稿仅用于描述应对方式偏好，不是能力测验，也不是临床评估。中文译本尚未经验证。

Your first round of tests did not go well and your usual studying habits are not working

你在第一轮考试中的表现不理想，而且平时的学习习惯没有起到作用。

A. Wait until the next round of tests to see if you’ll do better

B. Review your first round of tests once you get them back to understand your mistakes

C. Make and work with a study group with peers in your classes to better understand the material

D. Start going to office hours regularly for each of your courses
   开始定期参加每门课程的教师答疑时段。

E. Visit the student success center to learn a new study method
   前往学生学业支持中心，学习一种新的学习方法。

## Author descriptions / 作者描述

These are translations of Appendix D author descriptions, not empirical conclusions about a respondent. These general comments are not to be presented as validated personal advice. 下文为附录 D 作者描述的翻译，并非针对作答者的实证结论，也不应作为经过验证的个体建议。

### A · taking no action · 不采取行动

In response to obstacles, demands, and opportunities in engineering, you prefer to wait to see how something will unfold before taking action. The strength of this approach is that you have energy to use to support other aspects of your life within or beyond school because you are not responding to your immediate surroundings. The drawback of this approach is that the situation you are facing could get worse if you do nothing about it, potentially creating more problems for you in the future. Overall, not responding to some situations is likely okay, but a balanced approach is optimal where you seek out support for situations that are the most troublesome for you. Navigating engineering is hard and reaching out for support can make it easier!

面对工程学习中的障碍、要求和机会，你倾向于先等待，看看事情如何发展，再采取行动。这种做法的优势是，由于你没有立即回应眼前的处境，你有精力照顾校内或校外生活的其他方面。其不足是，如果你不采取任何行动，眼前的情况可能恶化，给未来带来更多问题。总体而言，对某些情境暂不作回应可能并无大碍，但更理想的是采取平衡的做法，对那些最令你困扰的情况寻求支持。应对工程学习中的种种问题并不容易，主动寻求支持可以让它变得轻松一些！

### B · independent troubleshooting · 独立排查与解决问题

In response to obstacles, demands, and opportunities in engineering, you prefer to support yourself by finding solutions to your problems independently. The strength of this approach is that you are able to use whatever you can find to help yourself through a situation. The drawback of this approach is that you are limited to solutions you can find and you may spend extra time finding a solution that someone else may already know. Overall, relying on yourself to navigate through tough situations in engineering can be useful to develop troubleshooting skills; however, it is also encouraged to seek out support beyond yourself when you face situations that are difficult to tackle alone. Navigating engineering is hard and reaching out for support can make it easier!

面对工程学习中的障碍、要求和机会，你倾向于通过独立寻找问题的解决办法来支持自己。这种做法的优势是，你能够利用自己找到的各种资源来帮助自己渡过难关。其不足是，你能采用的办法受限于自己能找到的解决方案，而且你可能花费额外时间寻找别人已经知道的办法。总体而言，依靠自己应对工程学习中的困难情境，有助于培养排查和解决问题的技能；不过，面对难以独自应对的情况时，也鼓励你寻求自身之外的支持。应对工程学习中的种种问题并不容易，主动寻求支持可以让它变得轻松一些！

### C · personal support · 寻求个人关系支持

In response to obstacles, demands, and opportunities in engineering, you prefer to rely on support from peers, friends, and/or family. The strength of this approach is that you seek out support from people who you know and are likely comfortable with. The drawback of this approach is that you are limited to solutions provided by people you already know, who may not be familiar with your specific engineering context. Overall, relying on known people to troubleshoot tough situations is engineering can be useful for getting personalized emotional support; however, it is also encouraged to seek out support from those you may not already have a connection with, such as university resources.

面对工程学习中的障碍、要求和机会，你倾向于依靠同伴、朋友和／或家人的支持。这种做法的优势是，你会向认识的人寻求支持，与他们相处时你也很可能感到自在。其不足是，你能获得的解决办法受限于已经认识的人所能提供的建议，而他们未必熟悉你所处的具体工程学习环境。总体而言，依靠熟悉的人应对工程学习中的困难情境，有助于获得贴合个人情况的情感支持；不过，也鼓励你向尚未建立联系的支持来源寻求帮助，例如大学提供的资源。

### D · academic support · 寻求学业支持

In response to obstacles, demands, and opportunities in engineering, you prefer to rely on support from instructors, teaching assistants, and/or advisors.
