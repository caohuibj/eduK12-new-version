## Interpretation safeguards

- Do not label respondents “closed-minded,” “irrational,” “high/low ability,” “pass/fail,” or clinically at risk.
- Do not produce percentiles, norm bands, diagnosis, suitability for employment/admission, or certainty about real-world conduct.
- Do not publish inferred dimension mappings or dimension scores.
- Do not use the highest-keyed option as individualized advice about medication, surgery, alcohol, financial commitments or elections.
- Medical, surgical and alcohol content is hypothetical stimulus text, not current clinical guidance. Keep this explanation outside the original scenario text.
- If a later approved research report displays any score, it must state the exact confirmed version, formula, missing-data rule, population, translation status and interpretive limits. No such approval or implementation is supplied here.
