## Terminology and ambiguities for independent review

- Actively open-minded thinking → 积极开放思维. This is a working construct translation, not a claim of an established validated Chinese measure.
- Preserve adult work/family contexts, ages, deadlines, percentages, counts and currency. Do not convert US dollars into RMB or substitute Chinese institutional systems. Final Study 4 is adult English-language initial-validation evidence, not K–12 or Chinese evidence.
- Item 2 daycare center → 日托中心; kindergarten → 幼儿园. The source's distinction is retained. Educators → 教育人员, available professionals → 园内可提供服务的专业人员. These do not imply matching local staffing rules. Montessori/Waldorf remain 蒙台梭利／华德福.
- Item 4 “alternative medicine” is rendered 替代疗法药物 because the options explicitly call it medicine/drug and describe buying it. This is hypothetical source content, not endorsement or a factual efficacy claim. Do not silently replace the response with a clinical consultation that the source did not specify.
- Item 6 “keep to the sidelines” rendered 待在一旁，很少参与. This independent-review correction avoids adding a voluntary unwillingness to participate; the source does not specify a diagnosis. “Plastic surgery” remains 整形手术, not a specific operative technique.
- Item 7 school psychologist → 学校的心理学专业人员; giftedness → 资优; class teacher → 班级教师. Do not silently equate with a Chinese 班主任, psychiatrist or clinical diagnosis. “Content and dynamics” rendered 内容、开展方式; reviewer should assess whether 运行方式 better preserves the program-process sense. A third more extensive remains 多三分之一, not a quantified increase in intelligence or giftedness.
- Item 8 preserves the tension between a possible surgery and the physician's reassurance. Do not change it into an instruction that surgery is necessary or that the respondent is ill. Long-term prognosis → 长期预后. Generic option pronoun “they” is rendered 她 in line with the explicitly female doctor in the stem; no new person is introduced.
- Item 10 monthly rent remains 15,000 美元; 15 employees and “most experienced” are retained. No real financial recommendation is implied.
- Item 11 “older colleague” is rendered 年长同事, not merely more senior; the separate longtime-employment phrase remains. The confident causal claims in option 1 remain as stimulus wording, not evidence-backed organizational recommendations.
- Item 12 retains unnamed fictional candidates A/B. “Apolitical” → 不关心政治. This is translation of a research stimulus, not personalized persuasion. Original “an advice” is retained in English and translated naturally in Chinese.
- Item 13 retains the original health claims as characters' beliefs and reported research claims. A source-keyed answer is not actual advice to drink wine, to ignore research or to self-manage illness. No fresh health guidance is inserted into the stimulus. “All previous studies” is preserved rather than weakened into “some studies”.
- Item 14 mother-in-law → 岳母或婆婆, explicitly preserving the English term's alternatives without inventing the respondent's gender. Source “Everyone who knows you know” remains unchanged in English. Chinese recipe/seasoning wording is naturalized, not culturally localized.
- Item 15 statics expert → 结构静力学专家; static resistance → 静力承载能力. These engineering terms need bilingual reviewer attention: the source does not specify a technical calculation or a legally authoritative safety determination. The grammatical “residential building” is retained in English while Chinese conveys the intended plural.
- Item 16 has an original gender inconsistency (his in the stem, her in option 2). English is preserved. Chinese uses 同事／对方 across these references, rather than inventing a change of speaker or resolving the source's gender inconsistency arbitrarily. “Junior” → 资历较浅, not necessarily young. “React vigorously” → 作出强硬回应; reviewer should check intensity.

## Score evidence limits

Author-supported ordered item values are 1/2/3, with Figure A1 a/b/c labels; no reverse items. The final mean formula sum/16 is a strongly supported continuity inference from earlier averaging methods and the final range, not a directly confirmed final-16 equation. Keep that distinction everywhere.

The sentence about a measure that “remained the same” on page 18 concerns a different AOT self-report measure; it is not proof of the AOT-SJT final formula. Do not deploy or display the provisional mean until the remaining formula gate is resolved.

Final dimension counts quantity 4 / direction 7 / overconfidence 5 are reported, but the item-to-dimension mapping is inferred. This draft deliberately removes the source package's per-item provisional mapping. Do not publish subscale assignments or scores.

Study 4 US Prolific N=173 sample statistics and internal-consistency estimates are study evidence, not norms: the source package reports mean 2.31, SD .21; omega total .68 and dimension estimates .53/.61/.40. No Chinese validation, clinical thresholds or population percentiles follow from these values.
