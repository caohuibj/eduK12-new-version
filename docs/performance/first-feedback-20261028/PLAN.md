## Four-entry decisions

- Survey GENERAL_ONLY: already inline on the first unambiguous FINAL. Preserve.
- Survey explicit DESCRIPTIVE: reuse its existing immutable-result projectors
  inside the first non-flow FINAL's current identity/consent/withdrawal locks.
  Return only after transaction commit; preserve AFTER_COLLECTION withholding.
  No score recomputation or saved cipher reread. First optional projection failure
  retains ordinary confirmation and the independent reader recovery path.
  Flow, idempotent replay, ambiguous retry, refresh and history retain current
  authorization reads. This slice does not automatically close those differences.
- Training synchronous Form/Scale, School and Teacher: their common renderer
  already gets a durable unit receipt, but its submitting flag hid COMPLETED
  while progress/report reload was pending. Prioritize actual accepted metadata;
  never equate a completed child with its whole parent.
- Training whole/worker: keep genuine ACK-before-READY and independent disclosure.
  No invented synchronous report, queue replacement or timing claim.
- Education report POST co-delivery is still open: receipt-only completed=true
  names a child, not the parent. Do not infer parent completion, borrow Training
  disclosure, or attach a report without its independent current authority.

There is no claim that one entry completes the whole four-entry capability card.
Shared renderer applies to its actual Form/Scale consumers; native cognitive/SJT
and separate Survey protocols retain their existing adapters. Further delivery
optimization must use observed costs and each real protocol, not copy one DTO.
