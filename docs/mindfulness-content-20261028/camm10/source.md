1. I get upset with myself for having feelings that don’t make sense.
2. At school, I walk from class to class without noticing what I’m doing.
3. I keep myself busy so I don’t notice my thoughts or feelings.
4. I tell myself that I shouldn’t feel the way I’m feeling.
5. I push away thoughts that I don’t like.
6. It’s hard for me to pay attention to only one thing at a time.
7. I get upset with myself for having certain thoughts.
8. I think about things that have happened in the past instead of thinking about things that are happening right now.
9. I think that some of my feelings are bad and that I shouldn’t have them.
10. I stop myself from having feelings that I don’t like.

## 官方计分说明（第 2 页逐字摘录）

> CAMM: Scoring instructions
>
> First reverse all scores by changing 0 to 4, 1 to 3, 3 to 1, and 4 to 0 (2 stays unchanged). Then sum all items. Higher scores correspond to higher levels of mindfulness.
>
> For more information, see:
>
> Greco, L., Baer, R. A., & Smith, G. T. (2011). Assessing mindfulness in children and adolescents: Development and validation of the child and adolescent mindfulness measure (CAMM). Psychological Assessment, 23, 606-614.

摘录说明：layout 提取中 "correspond" 后出现双空格伪影、页码区间断为 "606-␣␣␣␣␣␣614"，plain 提取为正常文本；上表按 plain 模式照录。官方计分说明即上列两句——**全部题目反向**（0→4、1→3、3→1、4→0、2 不变，等价于 4−原分）**后求和**，"Higher scores correspond to higher levels of mindfulness"；没有分量表、没有分维键，也没有总分行以外的任何结构。

据此得到的结构（单维，共 10 题）：

| 维度（内部键） | 题号 | 反向题 | 反向公式 | 范围（每题 0–4 求和） |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| mindful_awareness（单维总分） | 1–10 全部 | 全部 10 题 | 4−原分 | 0–40 |

单维之外不发明子维度；本条目以单维度 mindful_awareness 承载官方"全部反向后求和"的总分（0–40），不另设独立 total 键（与同批次 ffmq39/kims39 "PDF 无总分行则不设 total" 的处理不同——CAMM 官方计分说明本身就是单维总分，因此该维度即总分）。
