# CID-DUA3 | Classroom practice evidence guide

![A teacher and students use pictures, oral explanations and materials to approach one learning goal.](hero.webp)

Start with a lesson you can picture. Where did learners first meet the idea, how could they take part, and how did they show what they understood? This guide helps you collect a concrete example before choosing what to change.

## Use one classroom example

Choose one regular task. Keep its learning goal and try two ways for learners to engage with it or show what they know. Watch where they can begin independently and where support is still needed.

## If your notes are incomplete

If you have little evidence, save a note about what you want to observe next. Leaving a field blank has no rating or score. Read your evidence in the context of the group, timing and resources, without including student identifiers.
