These 102 original speech WAV files are byte-identical extracts, without clipping, normalization, resampling, pitch/speed edits, dubbing or transcription. Filename tags are the original actor-intention labels, not AI-inferred answers. Six non-neutral emotion categories, four formal actors and two disjoint practice actors were selected for this noncommercial research candidate.
